Welcome to Sri Sarguru

Where quality education meets compassion

Welcome to Sri Sarguru Matriculation School, where we nurture beautiful minds with love and care. Operated by the A.M. Muthuramaswamy Educational Trust, we began in 2000 with just five students and a simple belief — that love, knowledge and wisdom belong together in every classroom.

Today more than 500 children learn, play and grow with us, following the Tamil Nadu State Board syllabus from Pre-KG to 10th Standard, guided by caring teachers and a culture built on strong values.

Our Differentiator

A university diploma in Computer Science — by Grade 9.

Through the SUITS programme — the School-University Industry-Tie-up Scheme, in association with Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirappalli — our students from Std 5 to Std 9 learn skill-based IT alongside their regular syllabus.

On completing Grade 9, each child earns a Diploma in Computer Science certification — a head start very few schools in the region can offer.

  • Structured IT learning, Std 5 to Std 9
  • In association with Bharathidasan University
  • Diploma in Computer Science by Grade 9
  • Hands-on practice in our Computer Science labs
Achievements

Success our students can hold in their hands

From Sri Sai Olympiads to talent-search examinations, our students take part — and excel — in competitions beyond the classroom, returning with medals and certificates that build lasting confidence.

Moments that make our year

Tap any moment to see the photos.

National Days

Republic Day and Independence Day are marked on the school ground with the flag, the pledge and a morning of song and drill — the days our children learn what it means to belong to something larger than themselves.

Sports Day

Our annual Sports Day turns the ground into a field of races, relays and cheering houses. Every child takes part, and the day ends with medals, laughter and a great deal of red dust.

Yoga Day

On International Yoga Day the whole school gathers on the ground for a mass session — mats in rows, breathing together. It is one of the quietest and most striking mornings of our year.

Olympiad Achievers

Our students sit the Sri Sai Olympiads and other talent-search examinations each year, and come back with medals and certificates. The photographs on certificate day are among the proudest we take.

Say No to Drugs

Awareness begins early here. Our children make posters, take the pledge and walk through the neighbourhood with placards — learning to say no to tobacco, alcohol and drugs long before anyone offers.

Road Safety Patrol

Our Road Safety Patrol students help manage safe movement at the school gate each morning — a small duty that teaches leadership, discipline and care for others.

Graduation Day

Our UKG children graduate to Grade 1 in caps and gowns, with a stage, a certificate and a great many proud parents. It is the first big milestone many of them will remember.
